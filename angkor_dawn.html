West Outer Gopura
05:00 – 05:25 Waypoint 1

West Moat Pontoon & Outer Gopura

Tuk-tuk drops you off at the main western moat perimeter. Cross the floating pontoon in pitch darkness using headlamps. Pass through the triple-gabled Western Gatehouse into the main courtyard.

Surface: Pontoon then sandstone Lighting: Pitch Black (Headlamps)

Northern Reflection Pool
05:25 – 06:15 Waypoint 2

Northern Reflection Pool (Dawn Station)

Walk down the 350-metre raised sandstone causeway past the twin libraries. Secure a spot at the front perimeter of the Northern (Left) Pool to capture the 5-tower reflection before sunrise.

Civil Twilight: 06:05 (First color) Tactic: Depart pool by 06:15

Bakan Central Sanctuary
06:20 – 07:15 Waypoint 3

Bakan Ascent (Central Sanctuary)

Enter through the cruciform gallery into the inner courtyard while the majority remain at the pond. Ascend the wooden safety staircase on the eastern flank before the 100-person queue develops.

Dress Code: Cover shoulders & knees Stair Pitch: ~70° steep with handrails

East Gallery Wall
07:15 – 07:45 Waypoint 4

East Gallery: Churning of the Ocean of Milk

Descend from the summit into the 3rd enclosure's eastern gallery. Morning sunlight illuminates the 49-metre continuous carving showing devas and asuras pulling the serpent Vasuki.

Solar Angle: Direct low eastern illumination Length: 49 metres continuous frieze

07:45 – 08:00 Waypoint 5

Traverse Exit via East Gopura

Exit straight out through the rear door of the East Gallery. Walk along the shaded dirt causeway through the forest to meet your driver at the quiet East Gate access road.

Traverse Walk: ~350m shaded path Rally Point: East Gate road loop
