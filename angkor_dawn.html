West Outer Gate (Gopura)
05:00 – 05:25 Waypoint 1

West Moat Pontoon & Outer Gopura

Tuk-tuk drops you off at the main western moat perimeter. Cross the floating temporary pontoon in pitch darkness with torches. Pass through the triple-gabled Western Gatehouse (Gopura) into the sprawling outer temple lawn.

Surface: Pontoon then uneven sandstone Torch Requirement: Strictly pitch black; watch steps

Northern Reflection Pool
05:25 – 06:15 Waypoint 2

Northern Reflection Pool (Dawn Station)

Proceed down the 350-metre raised sandstone causeway past the two freestanding libraries. Set up on the front perimeter of the Northern (Left) Pool. The 5 towers mirror directly in the calm water as dawn breaks.

Civil Twilight: 06:05 (First color bleed) Sun Behind Towers: ~06:28 Crowd Strategy: Leave pool at 06:15 ahead of mob

Bakan Sanctuary Wooden Stairs
06:20 – 07:15 Waypoint 3

Bakan Ascent (Central Uppermost Tier)

Walk directly through the cruciform gallery into the inner courtyard while the majority remain at the pond. Ascend the dedicated wooden safety stairs on the eastern side. Access is capped at 100 people at a time; arriving before 06:45 avoids the line completely.

Dress Code: Shoulders/knees covered (Strict) Stair Pitch: ~70° steep; handrails fitted

East Gallery (Churning of Ocean of Milk)
07:15 – 07:45 Waypoint 4

East Gallery: Churning of the Ocean of Milk

Descend from the summit and walk out to the 3rd enclosure's eastern gallery. The early morning sun rakes low across the 49-metre carving showing 88 devas (gods) and 92 asuras (demons) churning the sea for the elixir of immortality.

Solar Angle: Direct low eastern illumination Atmosphere: Calm; tour groups are still at front

East Gate & Forest Causeway
07:45 – 08:00 Waypoint 5

Traverse Exit via East Gopura

Exit straight out through the rear door of the East Gallery. Walk down the quiet earthen causeway shaded by tall dipterocarp trees, passing through the peaceful East Gate to meet your driver on the forest perimeter road.

Traverse Distance: ~350m shaded walk Pickup Spot: East Gate dirt loop road
